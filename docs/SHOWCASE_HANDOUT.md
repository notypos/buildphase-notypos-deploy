# ClearLabel Showcase Handout

## What ClearLabel Does

ClearLabel turns NIH dietary supplement fact sheets into an evidence-first web
app for real consumer decisions. Users can ask plain-language questions, scan
supplement labels, check a saved supplement stack against NIH upper limits, and
create printable Decision Cards for clinician conversations.

## Who It Serves

- People comparing supplement claims at the point of purchase.
- People taking multiple supplements who need cumulative-dose warnings.
- People taking medications who need to know whether NIH names an interaction
  or caution.
- Clinicians who want patients to bring better-sourced questions into visits.

## What Makes It Different

- Answers are grounded in NIH Office of Dietary Supplements and NCCIH content.
- Evidence, uncertainty, and marketing claims are separated in the UI.
- Out-of-scope questions refuse instead of guessing.
- Dose checks are deterministic code, not model judgment.
- Health context is session-only; health conditions are never collected.

## Demo Flow

1. Ask: "Can too much vitamin D be harmful?"
2. Show cited evidence cards.
3. Scan a Supplement Facts panel or front product label.
4. Save a supplement to My Stack.
5. Run cumulative-dose and interaction checks.
6. Save or print a Decision Card.
